Joined, not glued.

In Japanese temple joinery, timbers cut precisely enough hold each other for a thousand years without a single nail. Enterprise systems fail where they are fastened, not where they are joined.

IronRim architects and operates the systems enterprises bet on: AI platforms, cloud infrastructure, and the observability, security, and compliance that keep them trustworthy. We cut the joints.

See the eight joints Commission the workshop

Duct tape fails. Joinery holds.

Most enterprise integration is fastening: a webhook here, a shared password there, a script nobody owns holding two platforms together. Fasteners work until load arrives. Then they shear.

A master joiner works differently. The joint is cut from the material itself, shaped so that load makes it tighter. That is how we treat every system we touch: the AI platform joined to the data that feeds it, the pipeline joined to the cloud that runs it, security and compliance cut into the frame rather than screwed on after.

Rare expertise, delivered with customer obsession, accountability, and timeliness.

Eight services, cut as eight joints.

Every diagram below rests open so you can see the cut. Bring your pointer to it, tap it, or simply scroll: the timbers slide home along their true path and lock. Each joint is real, named honestly, and chosen for the work it does.

AI & GenAI Engineering

The scarf that splices new timber into a standing frame.

LLM platforms, RAG pipelines, MLOps, and the guardrails and governance that keep AI accountable. New capability spliced into systems already carrying load, the way a temple's replacement beam is scarfed into the original and pinned with a single key.

On the bench: AWS Bedrock, SageMaker, Azure OpenAI, LangChain, vector databases.

Observability & SRE

The crossing joint that seats in plain view.

SLOs, alerting, incident readiness, cost visibility. Where beams cross, the watari ago hides nothing: every member visible, every seat inspectable. We instrument systems the same way, so nothing important is buried when it matters.

On the bench: Dynatrace, Datadog, Splunk, Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry.

DevOps & Platform Engineering

The dovetail: pull on it and it holds harder.

CI/CD that teams trust and platforms they build on. The dovetail's tail widens toward its end, so tension is what locks it. Pipelines and platforms should behave the same way under pressure.

On the bench: Jenkins, GitHub Actions, Azure DevOps, ArgoCD, Kubernetes, OpenShift, Helm, GitOps.

Cloud Architecture

The frame joint: post and beam become one load path.

AWS, Azure, GCP. Landing zones, multi-region resilience, migration and modernization. The mortise and tenon is the joint frames stand on, and a landing zone is exactly that: the joint everything else bears against.

On the bench: AWS, Azure, GCP.

Infrastructure as Code

Two halves cut from one template, identical every time.

Terraform, ARM, CloudFormation, Ansible. The okkake daisen is cut as two mirror halves from a single layout, then pinned so nothing shifts. Reusable modules, repeatable environments, drift-free automation: the same discipline in code.

On the bench: Terraform, ARM, CloudFormation, Ansible.

Security & Identity

The wedge that locks the moment it is driven.

IAM, network isolation, KMS and Key Vault, DLP, RBAC, secure AI workloads. Joiners call this the hell tenon: wedges spread it inside a blind mortise, and once driven it does not withdraw. Security should be cut into the joint, not clamped on.

On the bench: IAM, KMS, Key Vault, DLP, RBAC.

Compliance

The splice the manuals prescribe for sills.

FedRAMP High and Moderate, NIST, CIS, HIPAA, GxP, with Azure Government experience. The koshikake ari is the joint Japanese carpentry manuals specify for sill splices: not improvised, prescribed. Compliance is the same craft, the standard met exactly, every course.

On the bench: FedRAMP, NIST, CIS, HIPAA, GxP, Azure Government.

Data & Analytics

The joint that holds a moving part.

Snowflake, Databricks, data platforms and the pipelines that feed AI. The sliding dovetail travels the length of its groove and is captured at every point of the journey. That is the only acceptable way to move data: in motion, never loose.

On the bench: Snowflake, Databricks.
