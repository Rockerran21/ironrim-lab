How we work

Every engagement moves through the same four stages, in order. The sequence is the method.

  1. Assess

    We map the current estate, its risks, and what the business actually needs from it.

  2. Architect

    We design the target platform and the migration path, and put both in writing.

  3. Automate

    We build the pipelines, infrastructure as code, and guardrails that make the design repeatable.

  4. Operate

    We run, observe, and improve the platform, with teams working around the clock.

Company

IronRim is an enterprise technology consulting firm headquartered in Columbus, Ohio, with delivery teams across eight locations spanning UTC-8 to UTC+8. We design, build, and operate cloud, AI, and DevOps platforms for organizations whose systems have to work under load, under audit, and under change.

The practice is led by principal architect Prashant Chawla: more than 17 years in enterprise infrastructure, nine of them at JP Morgan Chase Global Technology Infrastructure, formerly an engineer at Amazon, with FedRAMP High delivery experience on Azure Government. His current certifications include Microsoft Certified: Azure Solutions Architect Expert, AWS Certified Solutions Architect Professional and Associate, Certified Kubernetes Administrator, and Dynatrace Associate.
